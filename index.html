The studio

Joseph Ghiller

Same address on Mosta Road for over a decade. The studio works in colour and in black & grey, takes on full sleeves and back pieces, and brings in guest artists through the season.

  • 2012 — the studio is already running on Mosta Road; a visitor names it in a Malta forum that July.
