Privacy policy

Inkredible Ghiller Tattoo · St Paul's Bay, Malta

This is a small website for a tattoo studio. It has no accounts, no shopping cart and no newsletter. We have tried to keep it that way on purpose.

What we do not do

  • No cookies. This site sets none.
  • No analytics. There is no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel and no other tracking script.
  • No form is stored. The "tell us your idea" form never sends anything to us. It writes a WhatsApp message in your browser and opens WhatsApp with the text ready — you decide whether to send it. Nothing is saved on this site, before or after.

The one thing that does leave this page

The page shows a map so you can find the studio. The map is drawn by OpenStreetMap, with map tiles served by CARTO, and the map library is loaded from unpkg. To draw it, your browser has to ask those servers for the images — and any request like that carries your IP address and your browser's user agent.

We do not receive that information, and we cannot see it. It goes to those providers as part of the normal request. If you would rather not load the map, decline it on the banner and use the "Open in Google Maps" button only when you want to.

When you contact us

If you write on WhatsApp, by phone or by email, we keep that conversation the way any business keeps its messages, so we can answer you and organise your session. We do not use it for advertising and we do not pass it to anyone else. Ask us and we will delete it.
